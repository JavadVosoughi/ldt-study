فرم رضایت‌نامه شرکت در پژوهشResearch Participation Consent Form

Participation Information (English)

You are invited to participate in an English language research study at Shiraz University examining how people recognize English words on a screen.

  • Procedure: The study consists of 3 short steps (about 12–15 minutes total):
    1. Background Questionnaire: A brief form about your age, education, and language background.
    2. English Placement Test: 25 short multiple-choice questions to estimate your English level.
    3. English Word-Recognition Task: A decision task on real and nonword items (with short breaks between sections).
  • Voluntary Participation: Your participation is completely voluntary. You may stop and withdraw at any time without any penalty.
  • Confidentiality: No personal names or identifying details are collected. Your responses are stored anonymously using only a participant code for academic research purposes.
